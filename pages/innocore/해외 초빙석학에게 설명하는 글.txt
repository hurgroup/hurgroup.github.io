The AI Frontier Exploration Research Center (InnoCORE) is a Korean initiative that brings together the nation’s four Institutes of Science & Technology to create AI-driven robotic systems for space, deep-sea, and disaster missions while training an elite cohort of postdoctoral Fellows.  Each Fellow is paired with an international scholar for virtual mentoring, joint papers, and pilot studies, capped by an annual workshop in Korea.  Participation places no formal obligations on the mentors; instead, it offers a flexible chance to build global collaborations with outstanding Korean researchers, and invited mentors will receive full travel support to attend the workshop and engage with the program at a depth of their own choosing.
