The AI Frontier Exploration Research Center (InnoCORE) is a proposed initiative that will unite Korea’s four Institutes of Science & Technology to develop AI-powered robotic systems for space, deep-sea, and disaster missions while nurturing an elite cohort of postdoctoral Fellows.  If funded, each center will bring together about 50 faculty members and receive ≈ USD 5.5 million annually for five years, ensuring ample resources and cutting-edge facilities.  We plan to recruit 50 world-class postdoctoral Fellows from around the globe, each paired with an international scholar for virtual mentoring, joint publications, and pilot projects, and brought together at an annual, fully funded workshop in Korea.  Participation imposes no formal obligations on mentors; rather, it offers a flexible opportunity to build global collaborations with leading Korean researchers, with full travel support for invited mentors to engage at whatever depth they choose.
