The AI Frontier Exploration Research Center (InnoCORE) invites exceptional postdoctoral researchers to help pioneer AI-powered robotic systems for space, deep-sea, and disaster missions.  As an InnoCORE Fellow you will join cross-disciplinary teams drawn from Korea’s four Institutes of Science & Technology, receive dual mentorship from a leading Korean PI and an internationally renowned scholar, and manage your own research budget with a competitive salary and full travel support.  Fellows co-author high-impact papers, lead pilot projects that feed directly into field trials, and present their results at an annual, fully funded workshop in Korea attended by global experts and industry partners.  The programme is designed to accelerate your transition to an independent faculty or R&D leadership position while giving you a front-row seat in shaping next-generation exploration technologies.
