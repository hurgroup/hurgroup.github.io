The AI Frontier Exploration Research Center (InnoCORE) is a proposed initiative that will unite Korea’s four Institutes of Science & Technology to develop AI-powered robotic systems for space, deep-sea, and disaster missions while nurturing an elite cohort of postdoctoral Fellows.  If funded, each center will bring together about 50 faculty members and receive ≈ USD 6.6 million annually for five years, ensuring ample resources and cutting-edge facilities.  We plan to recruit 50 world-class postdoctoral Fellows from around the globe, each paired with an international scholar for virtual mentoring, joint publications, and pilot projects, and brought together at an annual, fully funded workshop in Korea. As an InnoCORE Fellow you will join cross-disciplinary teams drawn from Korea’s four Institutes of Science & Technology, receive dual mentorship from a leading Korean PI and an internationally renowned scholar, and manage your own research budget with a competitive salary (≈ 65,000 USD) and full travel support.  Fellows co-author high-impact papers, lead pilot projects that feed directly into field trials, and present their results at an annual, fully funded workshop in Korea attended by global experts and industry partners.  The programme is designed to accelerate your transition to an independent faculty or R&D leadership position while giving you a front-row seat in shaping next-generation exploration technologies.
